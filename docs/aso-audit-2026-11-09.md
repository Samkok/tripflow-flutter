## 4. Top 3 quick wins (under an hour each, no release needed)

1. **Sync the Play full description with the iOS one**, then extend it to
   ~2,500 chars with the keyword phrases Play indexes: "road trip planner",
   "day trip", "walking route", "city break", "travel itinerary",
   "trip organizer", "group trip". Target each 2–3 times, in sentences. Keep
   the first 80 chars as the hook (Play shows them before "About this app").
2. **Fix the developer name mismatch.** Play Console lets you rename the
   developer to `VoyZa` today. On iOS the seller name is tied to the Apple
   Developer account (individual = legal name); a change needs an
   organisation enrolment (D-U-N-S), which is on hold until Xtremon exists.
   Until then at least make Play read `Kok Heng` or `VoyZa`, not `Samkok`.
3. **Add promotional text on iOS** (170 chars, no review, no search effect,
   pure conversion). Proposed:
   `Save your places, tap Optimize, and your day reorders into one clean
   route. Pins turn red when a place will be closed. 10 places free, no
   account needed.` (168 chars)

## 5. Detailed findings and fixes

### 5.1 Title and subtitle (iOS)

Keep the title. For the subtitle, `Maker` is the weakest word in the indexed
set. Test:

- `Multi-Stop Itinerary Optimizer` (30/30). "Optimizer" is the differentiator
  and pairs with the title's "route" to form "route optimizer", the one
  phrase no travel incumbent targets.

Run it as a Product Page Optimization test (title/subtitle are not testable
in PPO, so this is a straight swap at the next release; PPO covers the
screenshots below).

### 5.2 iOS keyword field (100 bytes, hidden)

Rule: no word already in title or subtitle (VoyZa, trip, route, planner,
multi, stop, itinerary, maker), no category name, commas without spaces.
Apple combines words across the three fields, so single words are enough.

Proposed (93 chars):

```
road,day,map,optimizer,vacation,city,walking,tour,organizer,holiday,journey,sightseeing,group
```

Phrases this unlocks: road trip planner, day trip planner, trip map,
route optimizer, city trip, walking route, walking tour, trip organizer,
group trip, vacation planner, holiday itinerary, sightseeing route.

If "optimizer" moves into the subtitle, swap it for `offline` only if the
app truly works offline; otherwise `weekend`.

**Extra indexed metadata at no cost:** the US storefront also indexes the
Spanish (Mexico) localisation's title, subtitle and keyword field. Adding a
Spanish (Mexico) listing with the same English screenshots and a second
100-byte keyword set (e.g. `planificador,viaje,ruta,itinerario,multi,parada,
mapa,optimizar,vacaciones,ciudad`) roughly doubles the indexed keyword
surface in the US. Several ASO vendors confirm this; Apple does not document
it, so treat it as a test and measure in App Store Connect's search terms.

### 5.3 Description

iOS (not indexed; conversion only): the first three lines are right. Add a
"How it works" block of three numbered steps above the feature list, state
the free tier and Pro price plainly ("Pro from $5.99 a month or $39.99 a
year; lifetime available"), and add the word "road trip" once since Play and
users use it. Remove "Terms of Use" link from the body only if App Store
Connect's EULA field is set.

Play (indexed): see quick win 1. Also drop "Anonymous mode" (users do not
search for it; say "no account needed") and "let your next trip plan
itself" (over-promise; the app plans, the user confirms).

### 5.4 Screenshots (the biggest lever, 25% weight)

Problems with the live set
- Frame 1 is the trip list. The product's one aha, the reorder, is not in the
  first three frames on either store. Store data: most users never scroll
  past frame 3.
- Captions name features, not outcomes, and two are internal vocabulary
  (`Customize Zone Radius`, `Proper Managed Trip`). `Google Map Integration`
  claims a partner brand in a headline.
- Dense dark UI on a dark blue background: at search-result size nothing is
  readable except the caption.
- No preview video on either store (autoplays muted on iOS; the demo clip
  `voyza_landing/public/optimize-demo.mp4` already exists at 720x1280, but
  App Preview needs 886x1920 or 1080x1920; re-export from the 4K source in
  `marketing/content-assets/DEMOS`).

Proposed set (captions double as indexed text on iOS since 2025; keep each
one a phrase people search)

| # | Frame | Caption (searchable) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Before/after map, tangled grey line → clean cyan loop, "24 min saved" badge | `Optimize your route in one tap` |
| 2 | Day chips + plan list for a 3-day city trip | `Day-by-day itinerary on one map` |
| 3 | Pins red/yellow/green with legend | `Know when a place will be closed` |
| 4 | Shared trip, two avatars, live edit toast | `Plan a group trip together, live` |
| 5 | Route from current location + transit line with fare | `Route from where you stand, by foot or transit` |
| 6 | Share sheet with route card + QR | `Share your trip with a code` |
| 7 | Paywall-free frame: "10 places free, no account" | `Start free, no sign-up` |

Design rules: light device frame on a soft gradient, caption at the top in
48pt+ (visible at thumbnail size), one idea per frame, each frame a step in
the story (save → optimize → walk → share). Reuse the vector redraws from
`marketing/tools/appui.py` rather than raw captures (Sam: raw stills looked
blurred).

Test frame 1 vs the current set through PPO on iOS (3 treatments, 7+ days)
and a Store Listing Experiment on Play (one at a time, 7+ days).

### 5.5 Ratings and reviews (20% weight, score 1)

This is the dimension that caps everything else: with no displayed rating a
listing converts at roughly half the rate of a 4.5+ listing, and Apple's
and Google's ranking both weight rating volume and velocity.

What exists: `lib/services/review_prompt_service.dart` fires the OS prompt
after 2 sessions, 1 successful optimize, 3 saved places, with a 60-day
cooldown and a 4-dialog cap. The thresholds are reasonable; the result
(fewer than ~5 iOS ratings, none shown on Play after 5K+ Play installs) says
the prompt is rarely reached or rarely converts.

Actions
1. Check the analytics counts for the sentiment dialog shown vs OS prompt
   issued vs sessions (events exist in `marketing/analytics-events.md`). If
   dialog-shown is near zero, the gate is too late for the actual session
   pattern (most users plan once, in one session).
2. Move the ask to the moment of delight: immediately after the first
   "X min saved" reveal, in the same session, instead of session 2.
3. Ask paying users at trip end ("Trip ended. Enjoyed VoyZa?") — the highest
   satisfaction cohort.
4. One-off: email the existing signed-in users (Resend, with explicit
   permission from Sam) with a plain "two taps to rate" link to the App
   Store write-review URL
   `https://apps.apple.com/app/id6758559163?action=write-review`. Ten
   ratings make the stars appear; twenty makes the number stable.
5. Reply to every review from day one (Play and iOS both show replies).

### 5.6 Metadata and freshness

- Release cadence is good (two updates in two weeks); keep release notes in
  traveller language (already the rule).
- Category: Travel is right. Add a secondary category on iOS: Navigation.
  Cheap, and the app appears in two category browse trees.
- In-app events (iOS): one event per season ("Plan your winter city break",
  "Spring road-trip planning") is indexed and shows in search; 31 days each,
  no release needed. Low effort once screenshots exist.
- Play LiveOps / Promotional Content: same idea; submit 14 days ahead.
- Privacy label: "Data Used to Track You" is a known install deterrent in
  the Travel category, where most competitors show "Data Not Linked to
  You" only. It is the price of the Meta SDK; revisit if the ads test fails.
- iPad: the listing says "Designed for iPad" but the iPad screenshot set
  reuses iPhone art. Either upload real iPad frames or leave it; it does not
  affect iPhone search.

### 5.7 Conversion signals

- State the free tier and the price in the description (done above).
- Once ratings exist, quote one real review in screenshot 7.
- Play shows "5K+ downloads"; the next badge is 10K+. Nothing to do but
  grow.

## 7. What this audit could not measure

- Search volume and current keyword rankings (needs AppTweak, Sensor Tower,
  AppFollow, or Apple Search Ads' popularity index; AppGoblin's free pages
  require a login).
- The live iOS keyword field and promotional text (App Store Connect only).
- Impressions → product page → install conversion (App Store Connect
  Analytics and Play Console Store performance). Pull these before and after
  each change; they are the only scoreboard that matters.
- Whether the Spanish (Mexico) indexing trick still works in 2026; measure
  via App Store Connect search terms after 2 weeks.
